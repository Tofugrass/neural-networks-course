Dear MICAH,

WHY WE ARE CONTACTING YOU

You started and saved a 2016-2017 Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) on the Web, but have not submitted the application for processing.

WHAT YOU NEED TO DO

You must complete your saved application, sign, and submit it in order for it to be processed.  To access your saved application, go to www.fafsa.gov and click "Login."  After you log in, you will have the option to continue your application using the save key that you created.

Your eligibility for federal student aid cannot be determined and your information will not be shared with your school(s) unless you submit your application.

WHERE TO GO IF YOU HAVE QUESTIONS OR NEED HELP

If you have questions or need help, visit www.fafsa.gov and click the "Help" icon on the FAFSA home page.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
